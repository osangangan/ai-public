## 1. THE EPISTEMOLOGICAL SHIFT: TREATIES AS SECONDARY, PERISHABLE "FIRMWARE"

### 1.1 The Core Insight
Diplomatic treaties (ECOWAS 1975, AU 2018, AES 2023–2026) are **human administrative instruments**. They are politically contingent, perishable, and frequently:
* **Overwritten:** When political alliances shift (e.g., Burkina Faso, Mali, and Niger withdrawing from ECOWAS to form AES in 2025).
* **Redacted / Annulled:** When domestic protectionism or military statecraft suspends free movement overnight.
* **Diverged:** When regional legal texts say one thing, but national border decrees enforce another.

*A Safe Passage* does not canonize ECOWAS, AU, or AES as eternal truth. The system treats them as **perishable "firmware patches"** tested against the **unyielding primary hardware of human and ecological reality**. When a treaty is annulled or modified, our underlying protocol engine does not collapse—it simply updates the secondary legal layer while continuing to measure lived ground truth.

### 2.1 Empirical Sensing Parameter 1: Non-Human Mass & Border Congestion via Satellite SAR
* **Technology:** **Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)** via open European Space Agency (*Copernicus Sentinel-1*).
* **Why SAR?** SAR emits microwave radar pulses that penetrate cloud cover, Harmattan dust haze, and darkness, creating exact 2D/3D surface texture reflections.
* **Empirical Parameter:** **"Border Chokepoint Surface Density (Radar Backscatter)"**.
* **How It Works in the Engine:**
  * High-density metallic reflection (high radar backscatter $\gamma^0$) at border crossings (e.g., Seme, Malanville, Paga) directly measures **queues of stationary haulage trucks**.
  * When a user shares a story of a 4-day customs delay, the AI correlates their story with real-time or archival radar backscatter anomalies proving physical bottlenecking.

---

### 2.2 Empirical Sensing Parameter 2: Human Luminous & Thermal Footprints
* **Technology:** **Day-Night Band (DNB) Nighttime Radiance** via *NASA/NOAA VIIRS* (Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite) + Thermal Infrared (*Sentinel-3 / Landsat*).
* **Empirical Parameter:** **"Cross-Border Luminous Continuity"**.
* **How It Works in the Engine:**
  * National borders are often visible from orbit as sudden drops in night-time radiance (electrification disparities) or intense focal points of light at militarized checkpoints.
  * The engine measures whether economic and human mobility is continuous across the border or severed by artificial border dark-zones.

---

### 2.3 Empirical Sensing Parameter 3: Free Movement of Data via RF & Telecom Tower Mapping
* **Technology:** **Radio Frequency (RF) Signal Bleed & Cell Tower Geolocation** (via open databases like *OpenCelliD* and ITU broadband spectrum maps).
* **Empirical Parameter:** **"Electromagnetic Border Leakage / Signal Overlap"**.
* **How It Works in the Engine:**
  * Radio waves do not stop at national borders. An MTN tower in Nigeria or an Orange tower in Burkina Faso radiates electromagnetic waves 15–25 km across the border into Benin or Ghana.
  * In border communities, people switch SIM cards or pick up "spillover signal" to communicate without paying roaming fees.
  * The engine models **RF signal leakage** as an empirical demonstration that electromagnetic data is inherently post-national and already defies political checkpoints.

## 4. WHAT HAPPENS WHEN TREATIES CHANGE OR DIVERGE?

Because the system is architected as an **Adaptive Protocol Engine**, it handles treaty evolution gracefully:

1. **Versioned Legal Modules:** Treaties are stored as versioned, modular policy plugins (e.g., `ecowas_1979_v1`, `aes_consolidation_2025_v2`, `au_afcfta_2018`).
2. **Treaty Divergence Tracking:** If Burkina Faso's AES passport rules diverge from ECOWAS entry rules, the AI highlights the exact divergence vector:
   > *"Under AES Directive 2025, your passage is supported by the unified Sahel passport; however, under current ECOWAS transit protocols, reciprocal coastal entry remains in diplomatic negotiation."*
3. **Primary Anchor in Human Rights & African Philosophy:** Even if every state treaty were repealed tomorrow, the system’s primary normative compass remains anchored in **Édouard Glissant's right to relation**, **Achille Mbembe's Afropolitanism**, and the **empirical ground truth of African human movement**.
