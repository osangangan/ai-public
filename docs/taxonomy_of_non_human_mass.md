# TAXONOMY OF "NON-HUMAN MASS" IN CONTINENTAL MOBILITY
## Conceptual & Technical Classification for *A Safe Passage*

**Concept:** Non-Human Mass refers to all physical, tangible matter (excluding living human bodies) that crosses or attempts to cross national borders. It encompasses the material Metabolism of the continent.

---

### 1. MANUFACTURED GOODS & COMMERCIAL MERCHANDISE (New / Finished Mass)
* **Description:** Newly produced commodities destined for commercial trade, retail, or industrial consumption.
* **Examples:** Factory-packaged food, textiles, pharmaceuticals, electronics, construction materials (cement, steel), vehicles.
* **Treaty Alignment:** Governed primarily by **AfCFTA Rules of Origin**, ECOWAS Trade Liberalization Scheme (ETLS), and national customs tariffs.

### 2. WASTE, RESIDUE & EFFLUENT (End-of-Life / Discarded Mass)
* **Description:** Discarded matter, industrial byproduct, or post-consumer refuse moving across borders for disposal, dumping, or recycling.
* **Sub-categories:**
  * **E-Waste:** Obsolete electronics, spent batteries, solar panel refuse.
  * **Hazardous & Industrial Waste:** Chemical runoff, toxic sludge, medical waste.
  * **Plastics & Solid Waste:** Non-biodegradable packaging, scrap rubber, ocean-bound plastic mass.
  * **Agricultural & Organic Waste:** Crop residue, livestock waste.
* **Treaty Alignment:** Governed by the **Bamako Convention** (African treaty prohibiting import of hazardous waste) and Basel Convention.

### 3. HALF-USED, SECOND-HAND & REFURBISHED GOODS (Circulating / Re-used Mass)
* **Description:** Pre-owned, partially consumed, or refurbished physical goods moving across borders in informal or secondary markets.
* **Examples:**
  * *Tokunbo* / Second-hand vehicles and auto spare parts.
  * Thrift clothing (*Katanga* / *Okrika* / *Mitumba*).
  * Refurbished smartphones, laptops, and re-conditioned home appliances.
  * Machinery and tools transferred between small-scale artisanal workshops across borders.
* **Treaty Alignment:** Highly contested at borders; often subjected to arbitrary import bans, informal levies, or security confiscations despite driving informal cross-border economies.

### 4. ENVIRONMENTAL & NATURAL COMMONS (Ecological / Fluid Mass)
* **Description:** Un-enclosed natural matter that moves across borders via hydrological, atmospheric, or biological ecological flows regardless of nation-state boundaries.
* **Examples:**
  * **Water & Riverine Flow:** Niger River basin, Lake Chad water mass, cross-border aquifers.
  * **Air & Atmospheric Mass:** Dust clouds (Harmattan dust carrying nutrients/pollutants across the Sahel), air pollution, carbon mass.
  * **Migratory Flora & Fauna / Livestock:** Transhumance pastoralist cattle herds (governed by ECOWAS Transhumance Certificates), wild migratory species, seed dispersal.

### 5. INFRASTRUCTURE, CAPITAL EQUIPMENT & TRANSIT ASSETS (Operative / Moving Mass)
* **Description:** Heavy machinery, transport vessels, and structural components that physically cross borders to facilitate logistics or regional construction projects.
* **Examples:** Interstate haulage trucks, freight containers, railway rolling stock, drilling rigs, construction cranes, power grid cables.
* **Treaty Alignment:** Governed by regional transport corridors, inter-state transit guarantees, and vehicle axle-load regulations.

### 6. CULTURAL ARTIFACTS, HERITAGE & ORGANIC MATTER (Symbolic / Ancestral Mass)
* **Description:** Cultural heritage objects, sacred artifacts, seeds, medicinal plants, and soil samples.
* **Examples:** Traditional artwork, ceremonial objects, indigenous botanical seeds, archaeological remains.
* **Treaty Alignment:** UNESCO conventions on cultural property, Nagoya Protocol on genetic resources, and national heritage protection laws.
